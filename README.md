## What this is

Most writing about AI agents is product demo or fundraising theater. This is the other kind — someone actually running a business with a fleet of agents and writing down what works. Three threads run through everything here:

- **Agentic commerce** — how buyers stop searching for you and AI starts recommending you instead, and what that does to a small business's SEO.
- **AI product discovery (llms.txt)** — why a store AI can't read is a store AI can't recommend, and how a plain text file fixes it.
- **AI agent payments (AP2, A2A, MCP)** — the protocols letting machines spend money and hand work to each other, explained in plain terms.

If you run a one-person operation or a small team and want the *why* behind these protocols before you touch any code, this is the right place.
